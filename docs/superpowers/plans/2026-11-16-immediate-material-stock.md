# Immediate material stock deduction

Approved behavior: sending a material request deducts physical stock immediately while the document remains pending approval. Approval must not deduct twice. Cancellation/rejection restores only stock previously deducted. Drafts do not deduct. Asset rules and permissions remain unchanged.

Design: narrowly scoped SECURITY DEFINER row triggers perform stock changes and append ledger entries only for a material line inserted into an authorized pending document, and restore net line debits on pending->cancelled/rejected. This lets ordinary users submit through their existing document INSERT/UPDATE permissions without granting general inventory UPDATE or ledger INSERT. Existing policies remain untouched. Parent/advisory/item locks serialize writes; every trigger change participates in the surrounding document transaction. No new flags needed: issue/reversal ledger net identifies prepaid pending lines. Approval checks this net and skips duplicate deduction; legacy pending lines without a debit follow the original approval deduction path. No existing records are backfilled.
